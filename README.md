# Court HR | Recruit for All Types of Court Positions

Recruit for all types of court position available to you on any types of tier, government, landless, and DLCs. Employ a high aptitude (good or excellent) courtier for your court.

## Installation and Use

1. Go To https://github.com/azrafebrian/CK3-recruit-all-court-positions/releases and find the latest version, and **download**.
2. Extract the downloaded .zip file.
3. Copy or move halfsade_courtiers folder and halfsade_courtiers.mod into \Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\mod
4. Open the game launcher and add mod to a playset, and enable the mod. 
5. Play game with selected playset.

## About This Mod

### Features

1. Recruit for a court position decision but for all types of court position.
2. Only recruits high aptitude courtier (good or excellent).
3. Easy access decision button in court menu.

### Requirements

Cost may vary depending on your tier, using gold or treasury.

### Effects

Get a high aptitude courtier for your court.

### Limitations

1. Cooldown for 20 days until a courtier arrives at your court.
2. Cooldown for 1 year for the selected court position after recruit.
3. Disabled in selector if court position is already filled.
4. AI will not be able to do this decision.

### Additional Info

- Replace the vanilla recruit for a court position decision.
- Available in a game that has already started.
